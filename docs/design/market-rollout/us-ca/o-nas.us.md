A great trip starts well before the first turn. Sometimes it starts with working out where the bikes will go, how to make room for everyone’s bags, or whether a roof rack really fits your car.

That is where MAKY.STORE comes in. We help you choose equipment that makes traveling with your gear more straightforward, so the planning can be about the trip rather than the packing puzzle.

## Equipment that earns its place

Our range includes roof racks and cargo boxes, bike and ski carriers, snow chains, coolers, tow bars and related accessories. The right choice depends on more than a product name. Vehicle fit, the mounting system, load limits and the way you plan to use it all matter.

We aim to explain what is included, what the product is designed for and what you need to check. When the details are not clear, we would rather help you check them than encourage a guess.

## Tell us about your plans

A family vacation, a weekend on the trails or the first ski trip of the season can each call for a different setup. Tell us what you want to carry and which vehicle you drive. A model year, body style and roof photo can be more useful than a long product search.

We are a Slovak company and ship from Slovakia. For customers in the United States, the available products, shipping options and total price are shown for the selected destination. Our [Shipping and payment](/us/doprava-a-platba) page explains how delivery and import costs are handled.

## Practical help, before and after you buy

We believe useful service means clear answers about compatibility, availability and the next step if something needs attention. We do not promise every product for every vehicle or an instant shipment of an item that has to be sourced.

Questions are welcome. [Contact us](/us/kontakt) and tell us what you are trying to make room for.
